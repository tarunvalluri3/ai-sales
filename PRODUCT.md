# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS, on the existing `create-next-app` scaffold. Greenfield build continuing on this stack.

## Users

Primary users are businesses (owners/sales teams) who configure and deploy an AI sales representative for their company. A secondary, indirect audience is that business's own prospects/customers, who engage with the deployed AI rep during a sales conversation.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered Sales SaaS platform that automates and improves a business's sales process. Businesses onboard their company, product, and customer/sales-workflow knowledge; the platform then runs an AI sales representative that engages potential customers, answers questions, qualifies leads, captures customer information, and helps convert leads into customers. Success means the business closes more leads with less manual sales effort, and the AI rep's interactions keep improving over time.

## Positioning

Full end-to-end automation of the sales conversation, not just assistance to a human rep. The AI understands each business's specific offering and context, then independently carries a prospect from first contact through qualification and information capture toward conversion, competitors that only assist or augment a human rep could not truthfully claim this.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- AI rep must be able to ingest/understand a business's product, customer base, and sales workflow (onboarding/configuration mechanism not yet defined — open decision).
- Core interaction loop: engage prospect, answer questions, qualify lead, capture customer info, hand off/convert.
- Continuous improvement of sales interactions over time is a stated goal (mechanism undecided).
- No CRM/integration, pricing, or deployment channel (web widget, chat, email, etc.) has been confirmed yet — open decisions for future work.

## Evidence on Hand

None yet. No real customer content, demos, testimonials, or case studies exist in the repo; future work must not fabricate these.

## Product Principles

1. Automation over assistance — the AI drives the sales conversation rather than supporting a human.
2. Business-specific understanding — every interaction is grounded in that business's actual product and customers, not generic scripts.
3. Conversion-focused — every capability (qualifying, capturing info, answering questions) exists to move a lead toward becoming a customer.
4. Improve continuously — the system is expected to get better at sales interactions over time, not stay static after setup.
